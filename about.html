About Us

Lanka Explorer is a student-built travel guide for Sri Lanka. It brings ten of the island's best heritage sites, beaches, hill-country villages and national parks into one place, with live weather, entry fees in rupees and an interactive map, so that a traveller can plan a whole trip in a few minutes.
